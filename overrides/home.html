We turned the ground into a sensor.

TactileArena is a modular sensing floor of 16,384 pressure-sensitive taxels at 2-mm pitch. It records where, when and with what force a freely moving animal contacts the ground, at approximately 1 kHz, synchronized with overhead video and without instrumentation of the animal.
