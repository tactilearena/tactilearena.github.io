We turned the ground into a sensor.

TactileArena is a modular floor of 16,384 pressure-sensing taxels at 2 mm pitch. It records where, when and how hard a freely moving animal presses on the ground, about a thousand times per second, alongside ordinary video and with nothing attached to the animal.

A real recording, at 0.25× speed. A rat crosses the 512 × 128 mm runway, seen by the overhead camera after registration to the sensor grid. Each glowing square is a 2 mm taxel loaded at that instant; magenta marks where a paw has just lifted off.
